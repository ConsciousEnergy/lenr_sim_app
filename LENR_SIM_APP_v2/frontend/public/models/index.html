LENR-Sim model registry

Crawler-readable registry of LENR/CMNS theories, Model 0 baselines, and epistemic status.

Comparative scientific workbench. LENR/CMNS refers to a class of reported anomalous thermal, nuclear and isotopic observations in hydrogen-loaded condensed-matter systems. The interpretation, reproducibility and underlying mechanism of these observations remain disputed. LENR-Sim makes competing explanations precise enough that they can fail.
