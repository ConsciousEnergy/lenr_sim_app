\section*{EQ-OPEN-RAD-01 (OPEN)}
A claimed suppression ratio $\Gamma_{\mathrm{ph}}/\Gamma_{\gamma}$ must be derived
from a concrete condensed-matter Hamiltonian. Harmonic or Franck--Condon
\textbf{upper/lower bounds} that fail a target ratio prove \emph{non-closure},
not a physical solution. Status: OPEN.
