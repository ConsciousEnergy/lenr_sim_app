\section*{ENG-RP-KM-01 (Approximate)}
Incompressible Rayleigh--Plesset dynamics are domain-limited when peak Mach
numbers are not $\ll 1$. Keller--Miksis includes liquid compressibility /
acoustic radiation corrections; the unified bubble--lattice path may auto-select
KM when Mach $\ge 0.3$. Making KM the default when warranted is deferred
(see \texttt{DEFERRED\_MULTIPHYSICS\_FIDELITY.md}).
