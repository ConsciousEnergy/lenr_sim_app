\section*{EQ-OPEN-EC-01 (OPEN)}
The free-space electron-capture threshold scale is $\sim 0.782\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
A condensed-matter proposal that supplies only $\sim 0.1\,\mathrm{eV}$ leaves a
deficit of order $10^{7}$ in meV units (Lean deficit identity). Status: OPEN
until a valid energy source is derived.
