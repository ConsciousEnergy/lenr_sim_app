# LENR PHYSICS SIMULATION PRD

## Overview
This PRD outlines the plan to implement accurate physics models for LENR (Low Energy Nuclear Reaction) phenomena in our simulation application, with a focus on multi-dimensional visualization and accurate representation of quantum tunneling effects.

## Goals
- Implement accurate multi-dimensional LENR physics models
- Create interactive visualizations that represent the underlying quantum phenomena
- Ensure physics calculations are performant for real-time interaction
- Enable researchers to gain insights into LENR mechanisms through interactive exploration

## Physics Models to Implement

### 1. Enhanced Quantum Tunneling Visualization
- Implement WKB approximation for tunneling probability
- Visualize potential barriers and tunneling probabilities
- Show temperature and pressure effects on tunneling rates
- Create interactive visualization of electron screening effects

### 2. Multi-Dimensional Lattice Physics
- Simulate metal lattice with defects and loading variations
- Implement phonon interaction models
- Create visualization of phonon-assisted tunneling
- Show localized temperature and energy distributions

### 3. Coherent Multi-Body Reactions
- Implement coherent tunneling models
- Visualize coherence length and multi-body effects
- Show reaction rate enhancement due to coherence
- Create interactive simulation of coherence effects

### 4. Energy Transfer Mechanisms
- Model heat and energy distribution in lattice
- Implement visualization of energy transfer pathways
- Show reaction hotspots and their evolution
- Create interactive demonstration of excess heat generation

### 5. Advanced Enhancement Mechanisms
- Implement QED-based enhancement models
- Create visualizations for surface plasmon effects
- Show Casimir force enhancements in nanogaps
- Visualize electron behavior in Rydberg matter

## Implementation Strategy
- Leverage existing physics models in the backend
- Create modular visualization components that can be composed
- Implement progressive enhancement for complex physics
- Optimize calculations for real-time interaction
- Use Three.js for 3D visualizations with WebGL
- Implement shared state between physics models and visualizations

## Success Metrics
- Accurate representation of theoretical models
- Interactive performance (min 30 FPS) during simulation
- Clear visualization of quantum effects that are normally difficult to conceptualize
- Ability to reproduce known experimental results
- Positive feedback from physics researchers
